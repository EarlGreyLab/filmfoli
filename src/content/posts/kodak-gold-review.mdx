Portra gets the wedding photographers, CineStill gets the neon crowd, and
Gold 200 quietly gets *everything else* — at a third of the price. For
travel on a budget, that math decides itself.

## Where it shines

Daylight, full stop. Warm midtones, honest greens, and a highlight rolloff
that makes harsh noon light — normally film's enemy — genuinely usable. The
dune grass frame (17A) was shot at high noon and still holds the sky.

## Where it struggles

Anything under ISO 200 conditions. Shadows go muddy-green fast, and indoor
tungsten light turns everything soup. This is not a night stock; bring
CineStill or push Tri-X instead.

> Rule of thumb: if you'd wear sunglasses, load Gold.

## The budget math

At current Lisbon prices, three rolls of Gold cost what one roll of Portra
does. Unless a frame is going on a wall, Gold's rendering is 85% of the way
there — and 85% × 3 rolls beats 100% × 1 roll every trip I've ever taken.
