Three years and roughly forty rolls later, the Olympus mju-II is still the
camera that comes along when I'm not "going out to shoot" — which is when
most of my favorite frames happen.

<PhotoGrid ids={["p09", "p04"]} caption="Both frames: mju-II, zero thought, full send" />

## What it gets right

The clamshell cover means it lives loose in a pocket with keys and survives.
The autofocus is quick enough for street work if you pre-half-press. And that
lens — sharp in the center wide open, with a gentle fall-off at the edges
that flatters film grain instead of fighting it.

## What it doesn't

The flash defaults to *on* every single power cycle. You will forget. You
will nuke a quiet café scene with a full-power burst and everyone will look
at you. Consider it a toll.
