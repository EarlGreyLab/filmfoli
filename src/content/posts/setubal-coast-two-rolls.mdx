The plan was simple: two nights in the Arrábida hills, two rolls, no phone
pictures allowed. The point-and-shoot rule for trips like this is the whole
appeal — you get *one* decision per scene, and the camera makes the rest.

<PhotoGrid ids={["p06", "p08"]} caption="Roll 013–014 · Serra da Arrábida · May 2026" />

## Morning at the campsite

Gold 200 loves this light. Everything before 9am came back looking like a
postcard someone's grandmother kept in a drawer since 1978, which is exactly
the compliment it sounds like. The orange tent frame (09A) is straight off
the scanner — no correction.

## The cliffs

Cabo Espichel at golden hour is almost unfair. I metered nothing, trusted the
mju-II, and it nailed seven frames out of eight.

Frame 36A — the last on the roll — was shot through a bus window doing 80
on the N379. Sometimes the film gods are kind.

## What I'd change

- Bring a third roll. Obviously.
- The lens cap incident: frames 28–30 of Roll 013 are beautiful studies in
  pure black. A point-and-shoot with a *sliding cover* exists for a reason.
- Camp coffee before first light, always.
